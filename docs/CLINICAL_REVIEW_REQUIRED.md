# Clinical review required before any real-world use

**Current status: NOT CLINICALLY VALIDATED — HACKATHON PROTOTYPE ONLY.**

This repository contains draft triage rules, draft interface translations, and software tests. Software tests do not establish clinical accuracy, safety, or approval. No clinical sign-off is represented by this submission.

Before any pilot or real-world use, the project must obtain and document all of the following:

- Independent review of every triage rule and every user-facing safety instruction by appropriately qualified clinicians in the intended jurisdiction.
- Review of all translations by fluent, qualified reviewers, including clinical terminology and emergency instructions.
- Verification of emergency numbers and service availability for each launch location.
- Privacy, security, accessibility, and usability review.
- Documented validation against representative cases, including false-negative and false-positive analysis.
- Written authorization from the responsible clinical and organizational governance process.
